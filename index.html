GET TO KNOW ME

ABOUT ME

In 2023, my journey into photography began unexpectedly. I attended an event where someone I knew was taking photographs. Out of curiosity, I jokingly asked if I could borrow his camera and take a few pictures myself. The moment I held the camera and began capturing what was happening around me, something clicked. I loved the feeling of being behind the lens and turning ordinary moments into memories.

Not long after that experience, I told him I wanted a camera of my own and decided to purchase one. From there, photography quickly became more than just something I wanted to try. I started photographing anything that caught my attention—nature, animals, plants, people, and everyday moments. Every time I picked up my camera, I discovered something new, and my passion continued to grow.

At the time, I was still in school and had never had a professional career or job. I was simply a young boy who unexpectedly discovered something he genuinely loved. I didn’t begin with professional training or photography courses. Instead, I learned through practice, experimentation, patience, and constantly challenging myself to improve.

As I continued developing my skills, I chose not to limit myself to one particular style of photography. I enjoy exploring different areas and gaining experience wherever possible. One of the biggest milestones in my journey so far has been photographing a wedding. Being trusted to capture such an important day showed me how meaningful photography can be. It isn’t simply about taking beautiful pictures; it’s about preserving emotions, connections, and moments that people can return to for years to come.

Photography has taught me to see the world differently. Things that may seem ordinary to someone else can become something special through the right perspective, lighting, timing, and creativity. What started with borrowing a camera at an event has grown into a passion that I hope will eventually become my full-time career.

My goal is to continue growing as a photographer, travel and experience new places through my work, and build a name for myself in the photography industry. More importantly, I want the people who step in front of my camera to receive more than just photographs. I want to give them an experience—one where they feel comfortable, confident, and excited to see themselves through my lens.

I know that my journey is only beginning. I still have much to learn, many experiences ahead of me, and countless moments left to capture. What began as a simple joke and a borrowed camera in 2023 has developed into a dream that I am determined to pursue.
